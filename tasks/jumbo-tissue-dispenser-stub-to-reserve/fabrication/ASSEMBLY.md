# Build and bench instructions

All dimensions below are millimetres in the shared **closed** CAD datum. Numbers in parentheses after a part refer to the generated CAD/BOM IDs. Use the drawings and `cut-list.csv` together; no cabinet dimension is obtained by scaling a screenshot.

## 1. Cut stock and prepare contact surfaces

Route the fixed shell and door from ABS stock. General cut/placement tolerance is ±0.10 mm; the cam profile, shoulder plane, guide pins, slots and slide stops use ±0.05 mm. The source door is 4×490×300.5 mm at X0..4,Y0..490,Z0..300.5. The fixed shell starts at X5, leaving the original 1 mm closed front gap. Side walls are Y0..4 and486..490; the top is Z296.5..300.5; the rear plate is X126..132.

The full original cut list includes every top/side/enclosure strip, the ten separate track/frame pieces, the three receiver pieces, three bottom bridges, all bench boards/legs and the six aluminium closing-tool pieces. `sizeXYZ_mm` is the **stock's own** XYZ size; the centre and rotation matrix locate it in the assembly. Do not use its rotated world AABB as a replacement stock size. The 24 source collision boxes for each support/shoulder are a geometric decomposition, **not** 24 loose timber bars: machine the equivalent regular 24-sided profile, 77 mm across flats for each 65 mm axle and 96 mm across flats for its 6.5 mm shoulder. The exporter independently verifies the equivalent boundary against the box union.

The three original receiver members are **steel**, as specified in the manifest and cut list; preserve their steel working surfaces. Make the added guide and spring seat steel too. Prefer machining `receiver_steel_body.stl` from a 114×64×32 steel blank at X12..126,Y186..250,Z256.5..288.5. This body combines the arm, lower member, guide and spring seat, with a stepped seat for the separate original upper member. The steel body and ABS cabinet shell are exported separately; the assembled `receiver_guide_spring_subassembly.stl` is an inspection reference, not an instruction to print a mixed-material part.

Machine the removable upper from 18×64×4 steel at X20..38,Y186..250,Z284.5..288.5. Its non-working mounting end sits on the body's 6×8 mm ledge X32..38,Y186..194 at Z284.5. Two M2×10 countersunk screws at `(X35,Y188)` and `(X35,Y192)` enter along −Z: upper clearance D2.2, flush countersink D3.8, steel-body D1.6 pilot 7 mm deep from Z284.5. Tap M2×0.4 to at least6.5 mm; the screw engages6 mm and retains1 mm pilot-bottom clearance. Select heads no larger than D3.8. **Install this upper last, after the bolt-guide washers and nuts are fitted.** Do not weld it shut over an unassembled guide.

Two rear M3×12 bolts with 1 mm washers at `(Y190,Z264)` and `(Y190,Z281)` attach the steel body through the ABS rear plate. Its rear clearance is D3.2 at X126..132; steel-body D2.5×8 pilots run X118..126, tapped M3×0.5. With the washers, 5 mm of thread engages the steel and3 mm remains before the pilot floor. Tighten to0.5 N·m maximum. These holes are outside the working cam and guide travel. A stock-and-weld body is an alternative only after milling mating pockets at all volumetric overlaps and restoring the supplied final geometry; whole overlapping stock blocks cannot be assembled as drawn.

Dry-fit the fixed pieces in a square jig. Join fixed ABS overlaps using a solvent-weld adhesive intended for ABS, following its cure instructions. If a seam meets only at a face, bond that complete face; do not rely on coincident mesh coordinates as a joint. Use the common fixture while the adhesive cures. The corner hinge reliefs, guide bridges, spring seat and keeper strips are manufactured features described below. Do not add hidden braces through either outlet, the roll paths, the moving cam or the robot's closing surface.

## 2. Secure the supports to the rear plate

The two support axes are at `(Y125,Z150.25)` and `(Y365,Z150.25)`, 240 mm apart. Each axle extends X56..121; the shoulder extends X121..127.5. Keep X121 as the roll's axial stop. Bore the shoulder and rear plate D6.5 along X. Each separate axle has a D6.5 blind clearance to X98 and a D8.2×12 insert seat at X109..121. Bond an OD8×12 M6 brass threaded insert into that seat with an ABS-compatible structural epoxy, a nominal0.10 mm radial bond line. Clean/prepare ABS and brass and cure to the adhesive maker's instructions. Do not assume that an oversized plain hole retains an insert by friction or heat alone.

Before attaching the cabinet to the wallboard, insert an M6×30 hex-head bolt (head height≤4 mm) and 1 mm washer through each rear clearance. Its head/washer occupy5 mm of the6 mm rear gap X132..138; the screw projects approximately18–19 mm into the support, engaging the full12 mm threaded insert with the remaining tip length in the clearance bore. Tighten at no more than 1.5 N·m into a sound insert, then check for zero visible support movement under a 10 N downward load at the axle front. The centre bore does not alter the roll-contact polygon or shoulder annulus. Do not glue the tissue rolls to the supports. The source's separate fixed support solids are thus positively fastened, rather than floating in space.

## 3. Assemble the captured selector

Fit the original ten frame/track pieces to their exact `manual_frame.*` cut-list coordinates. The front lower track is X15..29,Z−9..−7; its upper rim is X22..28,Z−3..0. The back lower track is X125..129,Z−9..−7, its upper rim X126..132,Z−3..0, and the original back wall X129..131,Z−9..−1. Both rails span Y8..482.

The selector is one connected ABS part: 110×224×3 plate at X17..127,Y13..237,Z−7..−4, with the exact thumbtab/bridge shown in drawing 03. Use the source selector CAD; its tab and bridge already overlap positively. Do not replace it with a bare rectangle.

The original open-sided rails alone do not provide the ideal joint's transverse constraint. Add the two explicit keeper strips: front X15..16.8 and rear X127.2..129, both Y8..482,Z−7..−4.2. This leaves 0.20 mm side clearance at both plate edges and stays below the tab bridge. Lower track to upper rim spacing is 4 mm; the plate is 3 mm, leaving 1 mm vertical play. The plate must translate without binding, with tilt below 2°.

Leave one endstop off, slide the plate into the captured rails from that Y end, then bond the endstop last. Its faces at Y13 and477 stop the 224 mm panel at centres Y125 and365, giving exactly 240 mm nominal travel. Gauge actual travel at 240.0±0.2 mm. The initial panel centre is Y125, covering the right aperture; the left outlet remains open. There is **no return spring, reset pawl, hidden actuator or automatic changeover**.

## 4. Machine and guide the real spring bolt

Machine one POM-C triangular prism, vertices `(X7,Y229),(X26,Y229),(X26,Y243)`, extruded from Z266.5 to278.5. The source 6×6 mm square load region X20..26,Y231..237 lies entirely inside that triangle. It is not a second protruding block. The original solid volume is 1,596 mm³; at 1,410 kg/m³ its mass is 2.25036 g. The actual incline is `atan(14/19)=36.384°`, as determined by these coordinates. Preserve both the incline and vertical load face; do not substitute a nominal 45° wedge. Cam flatness and coordinate tolerances are 0.05 mm. Deburr without rounding the working edge beyond 0.05 mm.

Two blind D1.98±0.005×3.1 bores at `(X17,Y232)` and `(X23,Y232)` enter the non-working top face, bottoming at Z275.4, leaving0.10 mm below each peg. Make two guide pins to drawing 02: D2 lower locating peg, D5 lower flange, D3 sliding shoulder and M1.6 top stud. Finish the lower pegs to D2.00±0.005 for0.01–0.03 mm diametral interference, and seat each flange against the bolt without closing the blind-floor clearance. Use POM pins for low added mass; the small top stud may be a bonded M1.6 steel insert. The mating D3.20 slots are in the 2 mm steel guide at Z280.3..282.3; slot end-centres are Y214 and232. Its mounting bridge is continuous with the machined steel body at X32..42,Y186..194; it is not an ABS part bonded to the steel contact members.

Assembly order matters: leave the removable receiver upper off; install the pin bodies in the bolt, bring the bolt/pins up from below the fixed guide into its slots, then add the D5×0.2 washers and M1.6 thin nuts (height ≤1.3 mm) from above. Fit and screw down the receiver upper only after the bolt travels freely. Keep the upper hardware below Z284.2; the receiver upper member begins at Z284.5. Pin lower flanges and upper washers leave 0.20 mm axial gaps on either side of the guide. Tighten the nuts against the pin shoulders while preserving those gaps; the plate must slide freely relative to the pins.

Nominal slots permit 18.20 mm centre movement. Fit 0.10 mm PTFE end shims to both slot ends, then gauge exactly 18.0±0.1 mm travel. These are real travel stops replacing the ideal prismatic limits. At zero travel the bolt's original coordinates apply; at full travel subtract 18 mm from all its Y coordinates. Pin/slot fit and end shims prevent overtravel and loss of engagement without enclosing or obstructing the working cam.

## 5. Spring and linear-guide resistance

Order or wind one stainless/music-wire compression spring: wire D0.50, mean coil D7.00 (OD7.50), 4.5 active turns, 6.5 total turns, closed/ground ends, free length 23.80. With shear modulus 79 GPa, `k=Gd⁴/(8D³Na)=0.39986 N/mm`; select a measured 0.400±0.020 N/mm spring. Its nominal solid length is 3.25 mm. At the full 18 mm deflection, length is 5.80 mm, leaving 2.55 mm before nominal solid height. Do not add preload: the spring just touches both pocket bottoms at q=0.

The spring axis is `(X18,Z272.5)`. The nominal outside faces are Y206 and Y229 at q=0. Each has a D7.60×0.40 locating recess, so the actual spring reaction planes are the pocket bottoms **Y205.60 and Y229.40**, 23.80 mm apart. Use those bottoms when checking free length; a 23.00 mm spring would leave 0.80 mm of dead travel. Only the non-working rear bolt face is relieved. Fit the spring after locating both pin guides. Check under full compression that it remains on-axis without buckling into the cam/cover path. Reject a spring that buckles or reaches solid height before the hard stop.

The source latch slider damping 0.8 N·s/m and selector damping 0.3 N·s/m are engineering targets, not properties guaranteed by machining the shapes. These physical parts use lubricated pin/slot and side-keeper interfaces; no invisible stabilising force is assumed. Keep lubricant confined to guides. For the bolt start with approximately 2 Pa·s silicone guide grease; for selector side gaps start with 0.05 Pa·s silicone oil. These starting viscosities are **not calibrated equivalents**. Measure the assembled mechanisms at the speeds below and adjust viscosity/film coverage; record the resulting values. Dry-friction values are likewise measured, not inferred from the STL.

For the bolt, use a force gauge along −Y and subtract the measured spring force. Measure at speeds 5,10,20 and40 mm/s in both directions. The slope of the residual force-versus-speed curve should be 0.8±0.2 N·s/m; the expected viscous residual is only 0.004–0.032 N. This requires a millinewton-resolution instrument; an ordinary coarse luggage scale cannot establish that coefficient. Record any stiction separately. For the selector, measure both directions over the middle 200 mm at 10,30 and50 mm/s: target breakaway/running Coulomb resistance 0.15±0.05 N and viscous slope 0.30±0.10 N·s/m. Adjust keeper alignment/film coverage rather than adding a return spring. If these targets cannot be measured and met, label the physical assembly uncalibrated and do not claim exact correspondence with simulation.

## 6. Install the pin hinge and physical angular stops

The hinge is vertical through `(X2,Y490)`. Two modules start at Z20 and230. Each has moving knuckles at `[s,s+15]` and `[s+30.4,s+45.4]`, and a fixed middle knuckle at `[s+15.2,s+30.2]`. Outer diameter is 8 mm; reamed journal bore D3.20; one stainless D3×297 shaft spans Z15..312. The fixed knuckles join the fixed side wall, and moving knuckles join the door edge.

The source door and case do not contain hinge barrels. Machine the explicit R4.20 relief around the axis in the narrow hinge-edge margin, at the opposite knuckles' heights with ±0.20 mm axial allowance. These are mounting clearances, not changes to the cam, broad closing face or tissue windows. The CAD already applies these reliefs. Do not print unrelieved source geometry and then force overlapping barrels together.

Dry-fit using the shaft as an alignment jig. Install D7.8/ID3.2×0.20 PTFE thrust washers at Z50.2..50.4 and260.2..260.4; these transfer door weight from the upper moving knuckles to the fixed journals. Mask the fixed bores against adhesive. Bond the shaft into **moving knuckles only**, and into the damper rotor; it rotates with the door. Fixed knuckles remain lubricated journals. The interleaved outer moving knuckles capture each fixed middle knuckle axially; the bonded shaft moves with that captured door assembly. Check axial end play is at most 0.20 mm and the shaft cannot pull through its bonded moving knuckles. Keep the adhesive fillet outside the original door broadface and controlled latch zone. No separate shaft collar is fitted above the damper seal cap.

Bond the closed stop at X4..6,Y0..4,Z80..100 with the door set to 0°. Its working face supports the original door inner face. Set the 180° stop at X4..15,Y486..510,Z95..105 using an angle fixture. These are passive hard stops, with no self-closing spring. Check free travel from 0 to180 and set the task's maintenance starting angle to90±1°.

## 7. Build the passive rotary damper

The supplied cup/lid/rotor/mount replace the ideal hinge's damping with real fluid shear. They are above the cabinet, outside the moving working envelope. The mounting plate is X−30..36,Y455..525,Z300.7..303.7, on 0.2 mm stand-offs above the case top. Two M3×8 screws with OD7×1.0 mm washers at `(X30,Y460)` and `(X30,Y468)` attach it to OD4.5×4 M3 inserts in the fixed4 mm top. Install those inserts in the nominal CAD seats using a verified ABS-compatible bond/fit; with this stack the screws engage3.8 mm and end at Z296.7, 0.2 mm above the insert bottoms. Both screw/washer envelopes are outside the cup: centre radii41.01/35.61 mm versus cupR31 and washerR3.5 give at least1.11 mm plan clearance. Fit the body to the plate by ABS solvent bonding around its base, keeping the shaft bore and O-ring gland clean.

Cup: OD62, ID51 cavity, 2 mm bottom, Z303.7..308.7. Rotor: D50×2, Z306.2..308.2, keyed/bonded to the rotating D3 shaft. Lid: 2 mm thick at Z308.7..310.7. This gives two 0.50 mm axial fluid gaps and a 0.50 mm radial rim gap. Install four **M2×4** lid screws at radius28, 0/90/180/270°, into OD3.2×3 brass inserts seated at Z305.7..308.7 in the cup. They pass through2 mm of lid and engage2 mm of insert, ending at Z306.7 with3 mm clearance above the mounting plate. M2×8 screws would collide with the plate and must not be used. Use a 0.2 mm silicone lid gasket seated flush in a 0.2 mm recess; do not add its thickness to the axial gap. Finish that shallow lid mating recess after printing. Seal the shaft with two 3 mm ID×1 mm cross-section O-rings in the CAD's ID3.2/OD5.6×0.8 glands. The lower gland is axially captured by the mounting plate. Capture the upper gland with `hinge_damper_shaft_seal_cap.stl`: OD16,ID3.2,t1.0 atZ310.7..311.7. Two M1.6×3 screws with **0.5 mm washers** at `(X−4,Y490)` and `(X8,Y490)` enter the lid's blind D1.25×1.70 pilot bores. Maximum screw projection below the cap is1.50 mm, leaving0.20 mm before the pilot floor; tap M1.6 without breaking through the remaining0.3 mm cavity wall. This retainer creates the actual0.8 mm O-ring squeeze cavity; an uncovered gland would not seal. Do not omit the seals or fill a bare open cup with oil.

For two Couette disk faces, `c = π μ (Ro⁴−Ri⁴)/h`. With Ro25 mm,Ri1.5 mm,h0.50 mm, dynamic viscosity244.5 Pa·s gives nominal c0.600 N·m·s/rad. Use a silicone-fluid blend specified by **dynamic** viscosity at 22±2°C; cSt is kinematic viscosity and requires density conversion. This calculation omits rim shear and seal friction, so it is a starting design value, not a calibration result. Fill without air pockets, close the flush gasket/lid and inspect for leakage while rotating through the full range.

Measure at 0.05,0.10,0.20 and0.40 rad/s in both directions, using a 100 mm lever and force gauge. Fit `τ = sign(ω) τc + c ω`: targets are c0.60±0.12 N·m·s/rad and τc0.015±0.005 N·m, with no return torque. Adjust silicone viscosity and O-ring preload/finish, not door geometry or hidden springs. Record temperature, breakaway torque and measured curve. Reject leaks, binding or a damper that prevents the task's safe manual closure.
